About this clone

This is a free, open-source web re-creation of the iOS app “Ink: Lockscreen Calendar, Note” by SilverAI Joint Stock Company — an app that turns your phone’s Lock Screen into a calendar and planner you see without unlocking. This clone rebuilds that core idea entirely in the browser: pair a photo with a clean calendar or agenda overlay, add your events, and export a wallpaper you can set on any phone.

Calendar overlayMonth grid with a customizable “today” marker — dot, ring or square.
Agenda & to-doYour next events listed on the Lock Screen with time, title and note.
Full customizationYour photo or a gradient, darken level, week start, colors and clock format.
Private by designRendered on-device with an HTML canvas. Nothing is uploaded, stored or tracked.
