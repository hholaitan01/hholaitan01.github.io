# ADUGBO: Street Kings — asset sources and licences

The machine-readable record is `public/assets/manifest.json`; `tests/manifest.test.ts` fails the build
if any file under `public/assets` lacks an entry, cites an unknown source, or is a raw FBX.
Licences were checked on **2026-10-17**. Re-check before each public release.

Rules applied: free and legally usable only; commercial use must be allowed; unclear licences rejected;
"free to download" is never taken to mean "free for commercial use"; no assets from FIFA / EA FC /
eFootball or other commercial games; no real club kits, crests, sponsor logos or anthems.

## Faces and the "Nigerian" question — limitation

The heads are **generic Rocketbox stock avatars**. Several have dark skin tones, but they are **not
Nigerian-specific likenesses and do not have custom facial features**, and the game does not claim
otherwise. No free library found with commercial-use rights offers realistic, specifically West African
face sets with matching rigs.

Legal workflow for original variations:
1. **Edit the MIT heads** in Blender (sculpt nose, lips, jaw, brow, cheekbones; repaint skin albedo).
   MIT allows modification and redistribution — keep the notice.
2. **MakeHuman / MPFB2** (CC0 exports since MakeHuman 1.1) to generate original faces from sliders,
   then transfer them onto the Rocketbox head topology or re-skin to the 80-joint rig.
3. **Commission an artist** (with a written licence or work-for-hire transfer) using photo reference of
   consenting models. Never use photos of real players or scanned likenesses without written releases.
4. **Hair**: the game supports full, high-top, mohawk and low-cut variants cut from the MIT hair cards;
   new styles (braids, locs, waves, low fade) can be modelled as original cards.

Any new face must be added to `manifest.json` with its author and licence.
