Who's building

I'm Olaitan — a fullstack developer who treats every build like an engineering problem: deconstruct it, design the solution, then ship with precision.

My flagship, StructSolve, performs structural beam and frame analysis with SFD/BMD diagrams and PDF export — and passes every one of the 52 test problems in Kassimali's textbook. That's the bar I hold my software to: not "it runs," but "it's correct."

I work end-to-end — backend APIs in Flask, Django, and FastAPI, polished React and Streamlit frontends, ML pipelines with XGBoost and scikit-learn, and automation glue with n8n. If it needs to be architected, built, tested, and deployed, that's the whole loop I run.
