LEICHTKRAUT — a full lifecycle email program

LEICHTKRAUT sells plant-based lymphatic-drainage drops in a category crowded with loud, over-promising supplements. The brief: earn trust first, then convert — without ever sounding like a hard sell.

I designed the whole lifecycle as calm, editorial German emails — personal letters from founders Dr. Bauer and Ava — carrying subscribers from first hello all the way through to reorder.

How I Design a Campaign Program

Whether it's a one-off launch or a multi-month calendar, every send is built to earn its place in the inbox.

01

Map

Plan the calendar or flow — what each send says, who it's for, and where it sits in the customer journey.

02

Angle

Give every email a distinct angle so a single product or offer stays fresh across the whole program.

03

Design

Compose in Figma — hero-first layout, brand-true type and color, mobile-first hierarchy, repeated CTAs.

04

Ship

Deliver production-ready designs, ready to build and send on schedule or trigger as automated flows.
